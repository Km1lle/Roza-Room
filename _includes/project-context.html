{%- comment -%} Retrouve le projet d'une page de la collection `projects` à partir de son dossier. Variables définies : _project_slug nom du dossier (ex. "exemple") _project_index la page d'accueil du projet (index.md) _project_pages les autres pages du projet, triées par `order` {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _parts = include.page.path | split: "/" -%} {%- assign _project_slug = _parts[1] -%} {%- assign _project_dir = "_projects/" | append: _project_slug | append: "/" -%} {%- assign _project_all = site.projects | where_exp: "p", "p.path contains _project_dir" -%} {%- assign _project_index = _project_all | where: "layout", "project" | first -%} {%- assign _project_pages = _project_all | where_exp: "p", "p.layout != 'project'" | sort: "order", "last" -%}
